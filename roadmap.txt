Skeleton loaders for #home-list/#explore-list/#profile-content while data loads — right now there's presumably a blank gap before renderHome() fires. A shimmering placeholder card (CSS background-position animation, no JS) makes load feel instant even when it isn't.
Optimistic UI on add/edit/delete — when submitVendor/saveEdit/deleteListing fire, animate the change into the list immediately (card slides in / fades out) rather than waiting for the Supabase round-trip then re-rendering the whole list. Feels dramatically faster.
Scroll-triggered reveal for Explore — since Explore isn't sliced like Home's .slice(0,8), animating potentially 50+ offscreen cards on every render is wasted work. An IntersectionObserver that only fires card.animate() when a card scrolls into view fixes both perf and polish.
Sticky search bar shadow on scroll — a subtle box-shadow/border that fades in once #home-list/#explore-list scrolls under the sticky search-wrap, so it doesn't look like it's floating with nothing under it.

Where a library genuinely helps

Motion — you're already loading it and not using it. Best use here: the profile sheet / edit sheet drag-to-dismiss (swipe down to close, with rubber-band resistance if released early) — that's finicky to hand-roll with WAAPI but a few lines with Motion's drag + dragConstraints.
SortableJS — drag-to-reorder the 5 photo slots in the signup form. Tiny (~14kb), no dependencies, drops straight into #signup-photos.
PullToRefreshJS — pull-to-refresh on Home, matches the mobile-first feel and is a natural companion to your bottom-nav pattern.
Toastify.js or keep your own — you said toast is already animated, so skip this unless you want stacked/queued toasts (multiple actions firing in quick succession).
