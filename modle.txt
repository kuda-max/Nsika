This is actually one of the strongest parts of Nsika if you pitch it correctly.

The mistake most people make is saying:

> "We'll make money from ads."

That's weak because it sounds like there's no real business model.

Instead, I'd explain that **Nsika is a local business infrastructure platform**. Discovery is the first product; revenue comes from services built on top of that.

Here's how I'd answer.

---

## The problem

Millions of small businesses in Malawi and across Africa are invisible online.

A tailor, mechanic, salon, carpenter, or food vendor often relies entirely on:

* Word of mouth
* WhatsApp
* Facebook posts
* Walking customers

They have no simple, searchable business presence.

Nsika solves that.

---

## Revenue Model 1 — Featured Businesses (Most likely)

This would probably be your first monetization.

Businesses pay a monthly fee to appear at the top of search results.

Example:

```
Search: Mechanics

⭐ Joe's Garage (Featured)
⭐ FastFix Auto (Featured)

Regular Listings...
```

Even if only a few businesses pay, this can generate recurring revenue.

Example pricing:

* MWK 5,000/month
* 500 featured businesses

That's:

**MWK 2.5 million/month**

without charging ordinary users.

---

## Revenue Model 2 — Business Analytics (Premium)

Free users get listed.

Premium users see:

* Profile views
* Phone clicks
* WhatsApp clicks
* Direction requests
* Search ranking

Example:

```
This month

Profile Views
482

Phone Calls
37

WhatsApp Messages
22
```

Businesses love knowing if their listing is working.

---

## Revenue Model 3 — Verification

A verified badge.

```
✓ Verified Business
```

Businesses submit documentation.

Pay:

MWK 10,000 once

or

MWK 500/month

People trust verified businesses more.

---

## Revenue Model 4 — Sponsored Categories

A company can sponsor an entire category.

Example:

```
Mechanics

Sponsored by Castrol
```

or

```
Building Materials

Sponsored by Lafarge
```

That's B2B advertising rather than random banner ads.

---

## Revenue Model 5 — Business Websites

Once a business already has its information in Nsika:

```
Name
Description
Photos
Phone
Location
```

Generating a simple website becomes almost automatic.

Offer:

```
Get your own website

gracetailoring.nsika.mw

or

gracetailoring.com
```

Monthly subscription.

---

## Revenue Model 6 — Booking (Future)

Some businesses take appointments.

Example:

* Barber
* Salon
* Tutor

Customers book through Nsika.

Vendor pays monthly.

---

## Revenue Model 7 — Local Ads

Instead of Google Ads,

sell advertising to local businesses.

Example:

```
Hardware Store

Sponsored Result
```

Only businesses inside a town pay.

---

## Revenue Model 8 — Government & NGO Directory

Governments and NGOs often need searchable directories for:

* Farmers
* Cooperatives
* Health workers
* Youth businesses

Nsika could license the platform as a white-label solution.

---

## Revenue Model 9 — API

Once Nsika has thousands of verified businesses:

Banks

Delivery companies

Insurance companies

Logistics companies

could pay to access verified business data.

---

# Why this business is attractive

The key is that your costs don't scale linearly.

Whether you have:

* 1,000 businesses
* 10,000 businesses
* 100,000 businesses

the platform is largely the same.

That's what investors mean by a scalable software business.

---

# The long-term vision

I wouldn't pitch Nsika as:

> "It's a business directory."

I'd pitch it as:

> **"Nsika is becoming the digital identity layer for local businesses in Africa."**

Today it's:

* Discovery
* Contact

Tomorrow it becomes:

* Discovery
* Trust (verification)
* Analytics
* Booking
* Business websites
* Local advertising
* Business intelligence

That's a much bigger story.

---

## If I were pitching Nsika in one sentence

> **Nsika helps local businesses become discoverable online for free, while generating recurring revenue through premium visibility, business tools, and digital services that help those businesses grow.**

That's a business model that's easy to understand, doesn't rely on charging customers to browse, and gives you multiple revenue streams as the platform grows.
